Limitations and ethics

What this research does not claim.

The non-claims are part of the work, not a footnote to it. They are stated first, at the same size as the findings, and without softening. Nothing here should be read as reviewed science.

Scroll to reveal

Prominent non-claims

Eight boundaries, each one measured against the record.

01

Not peer reviewed

No journal, conference or institutional examiner has evaluated this work. It is a submission candidate. Every result on this site is a candidate finding awaiting external evaluation.

02

Not production ready

Synthetic VLSI tasks only. There is no production design, no silicon and no deployment evidence of any kind.

03

No model independence claim

Two models were observed to operate under the tested conditions. Nothing more general follows, and no comparison of the two models is carried as a claim.

04

No superiority or equivalence claim

The single pre-registered inferential test was non-significant. Every other result is descriptive. The benchmark whose contrast saturated is reported as a metric failure, and that reading is explicitly not an equivalence finding.

05

No generalisation claim

Two deterministic authorities, six frozen synthetic tasks, tested conditions only. Nothing here supports a universal, arbitrary authority or production scale claim.

06

Causality not established

The behavioural question is closed at the level of association. The framing pilot leaned in the expected direction with a pooled difference of one third and an exact one-sided probability of 0.16265286, which did not reject the null.

07

Authorities are not interchangeable

Refuted by observation on one task: the same candidate drew ACCEPT from one authority and REJECT from the other, each correct in its own property. The structural authority is deterministic, external and read only, but it was developed inside this same research program, which is not the same thing as an independent institution.

08

Not the first of anything

Propose then repair loops, language-model constraint generation and model-assisted timing analysis are established prior art. This work claims none of them. What it claims is the particular separated arrangement and its controlled characterisation.

Vocabulary worth keeping when citing this work: under the tested conditions, bounded pilot evidence, descriptive observation, not established.

Material limitations

Every limitation that could change how a result reads.

Sample size

The first authority pilot used eight trials, the model comparison used sixteen with two per cell, and the framing pilot used eight per task and condition cell. These are small samples and they are reported as they are.

Synthetic scope

Findings are limited to a frozen synthetic benchmark of six held-out tasks and to the run-level records that were retained. No claim extends past that substrate.

Deliberately narrow breadth

Two models and two authorities. That was a designed boundary for a controlled study, not an attempt at a survey.

Architecture ladder

The partially supported rung was absorbed into the established rung, one hypothesis was not justified by the evidence, and two later rungs remain deferred. Deferred means not yet motivated by evidence, which is not the same as disproven.

A known floor condition

One task began from an initial state that the timing authority reported as clean without meaningfully checking it. It is documented as a property of the substrate rather than an effect of the method.

The saturated benchmark

The four-arm comparison ran 128 scheduled runs and every completed run finished with an identical reading on the primary metric, giving an exact probability of 1.000. That is a metric-power failure, not evidence that the arms were equivalent. The gate that would have authorised the next stage failed, and that stage did not run.

Literature positioning

The positioning rests on a documented adversarial audit with dated searches. It is not a systematic review and does not claim to be one.

AI assistance disclosure

AI was used in three distinct roles, and they were kept apart.

Assistance in execution and writing. Implementation, experiment runners, analysis and manuscript text were drafted and iterated with a coding agent under the author's direction. The research gates were explicit human review checkpoints, frozen records were hash pinned and no tool rewrote them. The author directed the research, approved every gate decision, and remains accountable for the content.

Models as the studied subject. The two language models are the object of study, the proposal generator under test, not hidden assistance. Their prompts, budgets, timeouts and invocation records are frozen and disclosed in the experimental records.

What was not AI. The evaluation authorities, the verification gate, the evidence normaliser and the provenance tracker are deterministic, version pinned, non-AI software. No AI system held the authority to accept an artifact, alter a frozen record or change a gate decision.

One correction is recorded openly rather than quietly: an audit pass during preparation caught and removed an unverifiable historical statistic before release. It is kept in the record as evidence that the verification discipline worked.
