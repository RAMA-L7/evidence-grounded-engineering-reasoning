Held back

Retained, and why

Raw run-level records. They contain provider account traces and full prompts, so they are held hash locked rather than published.

Model provider configuration. Private credentials are never included in a research package.

Benchmark answer keys, kept evaluator only so that reading this site cannot double as passing the benchmark.

Work from the later benchmark stage, which is documented separately and forms no part of the thesis claim.

Held-back material is not secret. It is available to reviewers under the access path below, and the retained hash chain is verified jointly with the requester.

Access

Requesting the retained records.

Run-level records are shared selectively with academic reviewers: identity and purpose stated, no redistribution without agreement, and joint verification of the retained hash chain. A useful request says who you are, what institution or role you hold, what you want to verify, and how you will handle the records.
